# Atlas materials

Original parchment, oxblood leather, and walnut textures generated for this atlas from the user's visual reference. Each texture is a 1024 × 1024 WebP used by the interactive Three.js scene; no external asset service is required at runtime.

The geographic polygons and rivers in `app/atlas-geography.json` come from Natural Earth (public domain):

- https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-0-countries/
- https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/10m-rivers-lake-centerlines/
- https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The country outline, coastal islands, archipelago, neighboring countries, and river traces are geographic data. The mountain relief and architectural miniatures are artistic interpretations, not survey elevations or measured replicas of the museums.
